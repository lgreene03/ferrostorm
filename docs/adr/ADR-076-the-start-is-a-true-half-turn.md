## Context

ADR-075 oriented the commander's placement scan to the map centre and bisected what still kept a Normal mirror from being its own half turn, using `mirrorprobe`, which compares seat 0's half of the world with the rotation of seat 1's after every step and prints the first tick on which they differ. It found four causes before the flow field's tie-breaks, each a rule of the sim that a human plays under as much as the commander does, and filed them as rows: P8-62 (cause 1, the opening hand), P8-63 (cause 2, the free harvester), P8-53 (cause 3, the flooring multiplication) and P8-64 (cause 4, the production exit). Its experiment, `docs/adr/ADR-075-residue-experiment.patch`, neutralised causes 1 to 4 together and brought income parity on `seatfairgate`'s sweep to 13 of 16 pairs in the frame it shipped.

This ADR lands causes 1 and 2, in that order, one staged commit each, measuring `mirrorprobe`, `seatfairgate` and the other gates after each so that every golden move and every change in the readings has one cause. Cause 4 (P8-64) was built and measured the same way as a third stage, and it is held back: with ADR-075's shipped frame it takes F8's binding first-launch half under its bar, and the evidence does not yet support changing that frame (section "Clause 3 held back, and why"). P8-53 is left to its own row and its own ADR. (Both have since landed together under D38: P8-53 as ADR-077 and cause 4 as this ADR's clause 3, after ADR-075's frame flipped; section "Clause 3 re-landed (D38)".)

All three rules share one convention, chosen so that each is the smallest symmetric rule that keeps the layout a base in the top-left quadrant has always had: **an axis on which the structure (or the start cell) stands short of the map's centre keeps the authored layout; an axis on which it stands past the centre gets that layout reflected.** A structure or start exactly on a centre line keeps the authored layout on that axis, the one tie. Because the rule reads only a position and the map's size, any two positions off the centre lines that are related by a reflection in a centre line, or by both (the half turn), get layouts related the same way. The tie is not exact for a pair: two structures or starts that are each other's half turn while both stand on the same centre line both keep the authored layout, so on that axis their layouts are translations of each other rather than reflections. That case is listed under "What remains asymmetric". The commander's scan (ADR-075) stated its canonical frame the other way round (reflected when short of the centre), which is the same symmetry off the centre lines with the opposite tie convention: on a centre line the commander's scan took the past-centre frame and these rules take the authored one. Since ADR-075's amendment of 2026-10-08 (decision D38) the commander's scan is in the sheltered frame, reflected only where an anchor stands strictly past the centre, so it now shares these rules' tie convention.

## Decision

### 1. The opening hand is a true half turn (P8-62)

`MapData.PlaceSkirmishStart` used to anchor each 2x2 Construction Yard at the map's `start` cell, so the yard always grew right and down from it. tools/mapgen.py rotates a start as one cell, so on every two-seat map the yard of the start past the centre stood one cell off the exact rotation of its partner's on both axes ((+1,+1) on all eight maps). The opening force was laid out towards the centre left to right only: the harvester always two cells below the start and the three rifle squads always two cells above it.

Now the whole hand is laid out in the start's own frame, per axis. On an axis where the start cell stands short of the centre the layout is as authored: the yard grows from the start cell towards the centre, the harvester stands towards the centre and the squads away from it. On an axis where the start cell stands past the centre the same layout is reflected about the start cell, so the yard covers the start cell and grows towards the centre (anchored `footprint - 1` cells back, read from the yard's registered footprint rather than a literal), the harvester stands two cells towards the centre vertically and the squads two cells away from it. Spawn order (every treasury, then every yard, then every force) and every entity count are unchanged.

**Four-seat maps.** The rule is a property of each start's position, not of a seat number. skirmish-09's four corners, and test-4seat's, are one orbit of the centre-line reflections, so their four hands are exact images of one another: the half turn for the opposite corner and a reflection for each neighbour. What the rule cannot express is a quarter turn alone: starts related only by a 90-degree rotation, such as the middles of a square map's four edges, would get hands that are reflections rather than rotations. No map ships one, and `mapgate` now refuses one by name (Architect condition C5, below). A start cell on a centre line (an odd dimension) keeps the authored layout on that axis, which is not exact for a pair on that line; every shipped map has even dimensions.

**What a player notices.** On the five maps whose start 0 is top left (skirmish-01, 03, 04, 05 and 06), start 0's hand is exactly as before. On the start in the opposite corner, the yard now stands one cell up and one cell left of where it did, its harvester begins above the yard rather than below it, and its three squads below it rather than above. On skirmish-02, 07 and 08, whose start 0 is bottom left, start 0's yard moves one cell up, with its harvester above and its squads below, and start 1's yard (top right) moves one cell left. In every case the yard covers the map's start cell and the harvester begins on the side facing the centre.

### 2. The free harvester comes out at the corner facing the map centre (P8-63)

GDD s4's refinery "includes one free harvester", and `World.SpawnFreeHarvester` set it down at the fixed point (anchor + footprint, anchor + footprint): the refinery's bottom-right corner point, whichever way the base faced. Two refineries that are each other's half turn therefore delivered their harvesters at corners that are not.

Now each axis is read in the refinery's own frame. Where the refinery's centre stands short of the map's centre, or on it (the tie), the harvester comes out past its far edge on that axis, as before; where it stands past the centre, past its near edge. And it is set down at the **centre of the cell** diagonally beside that corner rather than on the corner point. The exact reflection of the corner point is the refinery's own top-left corner, which `Map.CellOf` (a floor) puts inside the blocked footprint, where the harvester could not route; a cell centre reflects to a cell centre, and it is the convention of every other spawn path in the sim (ADR-011 clause 3, `ProductionSystem`, map-authored units). The clamp to the map is kept as a guard, but it cannot bind on a map more than four cells wide, because the cell beside a corner that faces the centre is always on the map.

**What a player notices.** Every refinery anyone buys still delivers one harvester, one cell clear of the footprint on each axis. For a refinery in the top-left quarter of the map it now appears half a cell further out (the middle of the diagonal cell rather than its corner); in the other three quarters it appears at the corner facing the map centre, so in the bottom-right quarter at the top-left corner, in the bottom-left quarter at the top-right corner, and in the top-right quarter at the bottom-left corner.

### 3. Produced units exit in the producer's own frame (P8-64): held back under D37, re-landed under D38

This clause was not in the pull request that ratified this ADR (PR 166). It was recorded here as built and measured, so that it could re-land as written once its condition was met, and it has: it re-lands as stage 3 of decision D38's pull request, unchanged in its rule (section "Clause 3 re-landed (D38)").

`World.ProductionSystem` sets a finished unit down at the first open cell of the fixed list `SpawnOffsets`, which starts two cells south of the producer's centre cell, then tries the cells beside that, east, west, north, the diagonals and three cells south; and an even footprint's centre cell is its bottom-right cell, because `Map.CellOf` floors the centre point. The unit then walks two more cells in the direction it came out (ADR-007's exit move) unless the producer has a rally. Two producers that are each other's half turn therefore set their units down, and walk them out, at cells that are not.

The rule as built: the list is read in the producer's own frame, reflected along each axis on which the producer's centre stands past the map's centre, and on such an axis an even footprint's centre cell is taken on the reflected side too (its top-left cell there), with `SetExitMove` handed the reflected cell and offset. A producer short of the centre on both axes, or on a centre line, walks the list exactly as it always did. `spawngate` gained a ninth stage for it (two half-turned barracks finishing a rifle on one tick), which goes with the clause; on re-landing that stage also gained a pair reflected on one axis only on a map that is not square (C6), and the clause's reversal names `pillarprobe`, which prints producer ticks held at 100 per cent with every exit cell blocked (C7). The search itself is one helper, `World.FindProductionExit`, which `ProductionSystem` calls in the producer's own frame and which `World.ProductionExitOpen` (observation only, for `pillarprobe`) can also ask in the authored order.

**Re-lands when** ADR-075's frame has been decided after P8-53 (ADR-075's own trigger for its frame clause), and `pillargate --bind` passes both halves on the merged head with this clause in it (D37's reversal). **Met on 2026-10-08**: P8-53 landed as ADR-077, ADR-075's frame was judged under C7 and flipped to the sheltered one, and with this clause in, `pillargate --bind` passes both halves at the head (60 of 72 matches launching against a bar of 36).

## Clause 3 held back, and why

## Compatibility

Both clauses that land change a rule of the sim, not only what a commander chooses, so they are the class Q024 (docs/questions/Q024-a-sim-rules-epoch-for-replays-and-the-lan-hello.md) asks about: a code-only change that neither the replay header nor the LAN hello can see, because no catalogue value moves.

**Old replays diverge.** A replay records the command stream and re-simulates it from the opening hand. Stage 1 alone rebuilds that opening differently on every map (start 1's yard and force on the top-left maps, both starts' on skirmish-02, 07 and 08), so a replay recorded before this ADR replays a different match from tick 1 and ends REPLAY DIVERGED with both hashes; it is not refused up front, because its catalogue line matches.

**Saves load and continue.** A save holds the world as it stood, opening hand and all, and no format, version or field changes, so a save written before this ADR loads into this build unchanged; the match then continues under the new rules (a refinery bought after the load delivers its harvester at the centre-facing corner). Nothing compares the continuation with what the old build would have played.

**A mixed-build LAN game desyncs and is not refused up front.** Both peers build the opening hand themselves from the host's setup, so two builds either side of stage 1 disagree from the first tick on any map where a start stands past the centre (every shipped map); the hello compares only the catalogue checksum, which does not move, so the pair is not refused, and the relay's state-hash comparison every 30 ticks flags the desync within two seconds. Builds from before ADR-073 are refused by its catalogue move, as before. This is acceptable only because no build has been published, and Q024's decide-by ("before the first published build") stands.

## Alternatives rejected

**Anchoring the yard so that start 0 never moves.** The yard of every start could keep growing right and down, with only the start past the centre moved, or start 1 alone could be adjusted. Rejected because it is a rule about seat numbers or about one map family rather than a property of a position: on skirmish-02, 07 and 08 start 0 is bottom left, so a start-0-is-fixed rule would leave those maps a cell off, and on a four-seat map it has no answer for seats 2 and 3. The rule taken moves start 0 on those three maps, which is the price of being right on all of them.

**Mirroring the opening force in y but leaving the yard where it was.** The opening force is the visible half of cause 1 and the yard the invisible half. Rejected because the yard's one-cell offset is what put start 1 a cell further from its own ferrite on five maps (ADR-075's per-map record), and the probe's first break stays at t=1 while either half is asymmetric.

**Moving the start cells in the map files instead.** Rewriting each map's `start` line (or tools/mapgen.py's rotation of it) so that the anchor of start 1 is the rotation of start 0's footprint. Rejected because it fixes the data for a 2x2 yard only, so a yard of another size breaks it again, it needs all nine maps regenerated and re-proved by mapgen, and the opening force would still be mirrored on one axis. The start cell is the right datum; the footprint is what needs a frame.

**Keeping the corner point where the refinery stands short of the centre** (clause 2), so that a top-left base's harvester would not move at all. Rejected because no exact reflection exists: a corner point reflects to a corner point, and `Map.CellOf` floors, so the twin of the bottom-right corner is the refinery's own top-left corner, inside the footprint. ADR-075's experiment found exactly that harvester unable to route (t=541). Any other point for the reflected side is not the mirror, so the pair stays asymmetric. Cell centres on both sides are the only exact pair, and they are the sim's convention everywhere else.

**Delivering towards the nearest ferrite, or searching for open ground** (clause 2). A corner chosen by the nearest field is rotation-invariant too, but it needs a field search and a tie-break on every purchase; a search for open ground is what the original comment refuses, because a scan's answer can depend on entity order and harvesters do not block cells anyway. The centre-facing corner needs neither, and on seven of the eight two-seat maps the home ferrite lies towards the centre from the yard in any case (ADR-075's record).

**Merging clause 3 with F8's first-launch half red.** Rejected: F8 has been met since P8-18 and `pillargate`'s first-launch half binds from D33, so a merge that regresses it with every automated signal green is exactly what the binding switch exists to prevent.

**Flipping ADR-075's frame in this pull request so that clause 3 could merge (route B).** The sheltered frame passed all three binding match gates at stage 3 (`pillargate` with 58 of 72 launching). Rejected for now because, measured correctly, it is level with the shipped frame on ADR-075's own measure and fails F5's start split, and ADR-075's frame clause has not yet triggered (P8-53 is open); flipping it would also move the commander goldens again without a sign-off on those hashes. Section "Clause 3 held back, and why" has the figures.

The alternatives considered for clause 3 itself are kept for its re-landing. **Rotating the exit order with the producer's heading towards the centre** (one of four compass headings, the list rotated to it): exact for a quarter turn too, but it needs a rule of its own for the heading on the diagonals, while per-axis reflection is the symmetry every shipped map has and the convention of clauses 1 and 2. **Reflecting the list but not the centre cell, or the centre cell but not the list**: each fixes half, because `CellOf` floors an even footprint's centre to its bottom-right cell. **Reflecting every caller of `SpawnOffsets` in the same stage** (the Carrier's unload, the decoy army, the tunnel deployment): left out for scope, since `mirrorprobe` never reached them as a first break and each has its own gate.
